# Design System

This document outlines the core aesthetic and functional principles of our design system.

## Theme Overview

Our current theme emphasizes a **dark** interface.

### Color Palette

The color palette is built around specific hex values for precise brand representation:
- **Primary Color:** `#00F0FF` (A vibrant, electric cyan for key interactions)
- **Secondary Color:** `#42CF8B` (A fresh green for supporting elements and energetic accents)
- **Tertiary Color:** `#1E293B` (A dark blue-gray for accents and depth)
- **Neutral Color:** `#0A0A0B` (A deep foundation for the interface, enabling high-contrast accents)

### Typography

We use a curated selection of fonts to ensure readability and brand consistency:
- **Headlines:** `Manrope` - Chosen for its modern and geometric feel, perfect for impactful headings.
- **Body Text:** `Inter` - A highly legible and versatile typeface for all general text content.
- **Labels:** `Inter` - Consistent with body text for clear and functional labels.

### Shape and Form

Our UI elements feature **maximum, pill-shaped** roundedness, giving them a soft and approachable appearance.

### Spacing

The design adopts a **normal** spacing convention, providing a balanced and comfortable visual experience.
